    Model Validation

    Compares predicted voxel units against observed BH layer positions.

    Unit Similarity

    Flags geological units with similar descriptions that may be duplicates or sub-divisions of the same material.

    Formation Pinch-outs

    Detects where formations wedge out laterally — columns where a unit is present adjacent to columns where it is absent.

    Optimal Drilling Recommendation

    Identifies the most informative borehole locations by combining model uncertainty with concept-geometry predictions and existing BH coverage.

    Sequence Stratigraphy

    Identifies geological sequence boundaries where temporally ordered concepts predict unit transitions. Derived from concept temporal ordering and model voxel contacts.

    Predictive Borehole Log

    Generate a synthetic borehole log at any plan location from the neural implicit field. Shows predicted unit sequence, certainty, and concept context at each depth. Compare to the nearest real borehole.

    Concept-Annotated Cross-Section

    Slice the 3D model at any azimuth. Concept embeddings are automatically projected onto the section plane — channel morphology adds a concave-up arc, fault geometry adds a stepped line, orientation axes annotate the cut direction.

    Geotechnical Risk

    Identifies settlement, bearing, liquefaction and uncertainty hazards from the model.

    Investigation Planning

    Identify locations where new boreholes would most reduce model uncertainty. Based on per-column classification entropy and distance from existing BH data.
